Pizza Dough

  • 00 Tipo Flour
  • Water
  • Active Dry Yeast
  • Salt
  1. Combine the water and yeast. Let bloom for 10 minutes.
  2. Whisk together salt and flour.
  3. Slowly stir together the wet and dry ingredients. Let sit overnight.
