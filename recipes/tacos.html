Tacos

Veggies tacos with a hint of squeezed lime juice.

Street Tacos

  • Flank Steak
  • Corn Tortillas
  • White onion
  • Lime juice
  1. Sear flank steak in a cast iron pan.
  2. Heat tortillas over stove and drape over a rolling pin to shape.
  3. Chop onion and sprinkle over tacos.
  4. Squeeze lime juice and enjoy!
